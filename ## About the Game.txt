## About the Game

This game was created as a project to showcase my **narrative skills in Digital Media**. Rather than focusing heavily on complex mechanics, features, or technical systems, I chose to put most of my effort into **storytelling, atmosphere, and the character's emotional journey**.

The project follows a young prince who has lived his entire life under the protection and shadow of his father. He knows very little about the world beyond his kingdom and has never had to face life on his own.

Everything changes when invaders arrive. His father falls, the kingdom is destroyed, and the prince is suddenly forced to face a world he knows nothing about.

Although the game may not be technically sophisticated, I invested a significant amount of time into creating it because I wanted to tell a story rather than simply produce a video or animation, which was another approach suggested for the project.

The story also reflects part of my own experience. When I first came to Ireland, I found myself in an unfamiliar environment without the people and surroundings I had previously relied on. Like the protagonist, I had to learn how to navigate a new world and become more independent.

The game is ultimately about **growing through loss, unfamiliarity, and experience**. The prince begins his journey knowing almost nothing about the world, but as he experiences hardship and meets new people, he gradually learns what it means to live beyond the protection of his father's shadow.

By the end, there is no perfect resolution. **Life simply continues.**

And perhaps that is the lesson: sometimes we don't become stronger because we are ready to, but because life gives us no choice but to move forward.
